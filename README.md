# Veilreach / 帷境远界

Veilreach is a Fabric 1.21.11 adventure dimension mod prototype centered on **The Hushlands**, a silver-night mirror wilderness of fog basins, crystal roots, ruined observatories, and sealed deep zones.

## Theme Snapshot
- English title: `Veilreach: The Hushlands`
- Chinese title: `帷境远界：寂辉荒原`
- Elevator pitch: Step through a woven moonlit gate into a silent otherworld where drifting silver fog, star-fed ruins, and sealed inner wilds create an exploration-first progression loop.

## MVP Direction
- Phase 1: Mod skeleton, registries, placeholder content, dimension keys, portal ritual items and blocks.
- Phase 2: Working dimension entry, atmosphere setup, initial biome and terrain palette.
- Phase 3: Mobs, ruins, progression resources, gear, and a mini-boss.
- Phase 4: Hazard-gated deeper region, boss drop unlock, and stronger equipment.

## Visual Language
- Dominant colors: mist silver, desaturated teal, pale moon-blue, black-violet stone.
- Materials: woven portal fibers, polished mirrorstone, hushwood timber, glow-moss flora, starglass relics.
- Placeholder art rule: bold silhouettes, limited 16x16 palette, readable contrast in low light.

## Planned Progression
1. Build a `Moonwoven Frame` in the overworld.
2. Use an `Astral Sigil` to open a Hush Gate.
3. Gather `Veil Shards`, `Hushberries`, and `Lunar Fiber` in the outer zone.
4. Craft early survival gear and fog resistance tools.
5. Unlock sealed ruins and push toward the inner silver basin.
6. Defeat the first guardian to reach the deeper district.

## Art Continuation Guide
- Blocks should look worn, luminous, and slightly ceremonial rather than lush fairy-forest fantasy.
- Foliage should read as windless and hushed, with cold colors and sparse highlights.
- Ruins should feel astronomical and ritualistic instead of medieval.
